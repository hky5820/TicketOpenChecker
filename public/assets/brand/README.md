# Ticket Open icon

Original artwork: `ticket-open-master.png`, refined with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-29. A cobalt admission ticket with unmistakable side notches, two printed-information bars and a lifted periwinkle tear-off stub makes the ticket-opening theme explicit while preserving the previous emblem's white-and-blue palette. No external logo or stock artwork is used.

Run `node scripts/make-icons.js` from the repository root to regenerate the six PNG assets with installed Chrome. The 192/512px general icons and 16/32px favicons have transparent rounded corners. Apple touch and Android maskable icons retain opaque square backgrounds so the OS can apply its own shape. The Android version adds 6% white padding on each edge to protect the lifted stub; smaller icons retain the larger mark for legibility. Header and notifications use the same artwork.

Android artwork must remain inside the central circle with radius 40% of image width: [maskable icon guidance](https://web.dev/articles/maskable-icon). The PNGs are committed; deployment does not need image generation.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Refine the referenced Ticket Open icon. The user LIKES its beautiful white-and-cobalt visual identity, bold rounded geometry and light-blue lifting corner, but cannot recognize a ticket or ticket opening. Keep that appealing visual language while making the subject immediately recognizable as an ADMISSION TICKET BEING OPENED. The final emblem must unmistakably look like a ticket, never like an O, a letter, a door or an abstract software logo. Replace the hollow loop with a SOLID vivid cobalt #3154F5 admission-ticket body, a compact horizontal rectangle with generous rounded corners, approximately 1.55 times as wide as tall. A pronounced concave semicircular notch midway along the left short edge, and a matching notch in the right short edge of the attached tear-off stub, establish the familiar ticket silhouette. Define the tear-off stub near the right quarter using a short white perforation with just TWO bold white slots. The periwinkle #9DB5FF right-hand stub is lifting upward and outward from the perforation like a ticket being opened, with one elegant narrow white gap between it and the cobalt body, physically aligned so both pieces still read as a single ticket. This lifted stub is the memorable design detail from the reference. Put only TWO short thick rounded WHITE parallel printed-information bars in the large blue left section, one longer above and one shorter below, with ample spacing; these are simple shapes, not letters. NO central hole. NO clock, calendar, bell, checkmark, arrow, sparkles, stars, badge or text. Use a very slight overall upward-right inclination for energy, but keep the ticket easy to recognize. Minimal flat solid-color geometry, luxurious proportions, clear silhouette, clean white negative space, no 3D, no gradient, no glow, no shadow, no texture, no thin outlines. Preserve the reference's pure white full-bleed square background and vivid blue palette. Do not put a colored tile or a border behind the ticket. Make the ticket and lifted stub together occupy roughly 65 percent of the square width and 55 percent of its height, visually centered; keep every meaningful edge inside a centered circle with radius 39 percent of the image width. No rounded outer canvas corners. One finished standalone square app icon, not a presentation board. It must be both aesthetically refined and obviously about opening tickets at 24px.
