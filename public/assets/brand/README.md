# Ticket Open icon

Original artwork: `ticket-open-master.png`, generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-29. A compact cobalt admission ticket with a large white notification bell communicates ticket-opening alerts. White background, rounded ticket notches and a subtle periwinkle folded corner preserve the visual identity. No external logo or stock artwork is used.

Run `node scripts/make-icons.js` from the repository root to regenerate the six PNG assets with installed Chrome. The 192/512px general icons and 16/32px favicons have transparent rounded corners. Apple touch and Android maskable icons retain opaque square backgrounds so the OS can apply its own shape. The Android version adds 6% white padding on each edge to protect the ticket corners; smaller icons retain the larger mark for legibility. Header and notifications use the same artwork.

Android artwork must remain inside the central circle with radius 40% of image width: [maskable icon guidance](https://web.dev/articles/maskable-icon). The PNGs are committed; deployment does not need image generation.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a new, exceptionally polished app launcher icon for a Korean service that tells people WHEN TICKET SALES OPEN and alerts them before they miss a sale. The icon must instantly communicate TICKET ALERT, not a used ticket, torn paper, door, abstract letter or generic ticket marketplace. Use the provided image only as a palette reference: white and vivid cobalt blue. Replace its composition entirely. Design ONE compact unified emblem: a bold cobalt-blue admission pass with a large simple WHITE NOTIFICATION BELL carved into its center as negative space. The ticket is nearly square, slightly wider than tall (about 1.16:1), with beautifully rounded corners and clear semicircular ticket notches in the middle of the left and right edges. The ticket body is solid rich #3154F5. The bell cutout is large, centered, perfectly upright, with a rounded dome, gently flared bottom rim and one distinct small round clapper below. The white bell uses confident filled geometry, no outline, no tiny detail. This must read immediately as a ticket that will notify you, even at 24 pixels. Keep the design composed and iconic, not clip art: geometric precision, generous spacing, balanced optical weight, one continuous blue outer shape, one carefully drawn white bell. Use a small inset section of soft periwinkle blue at the ticket's upper right as a subtle folded-corner detail, no detached piece, while keeping the overall ticket silhouette intact. No extra white text bars or dotted perforation. Pure WHITE full-bleed square background. Foreground mark roughly 64 percent of canvas width and 56 percent height, all artwork inside a centered circle of radius 39 percent of canvas width. Main ticket stays level and stable, no diagonal rotation. Keep the bell generously spaced from ticket edges. No clock, calendar, check mark, letters, words, numbers, sparkles, arrows, sound-wave strokes, floating badges, gradients, shadow, glass, bevel, texture or 3D. Only cobalt, a tiny periwinkle corner accent, and white. Contemporary Japanese / Swiss brand identity quality, calm yet immediately noticeable. Do not round the OUTER canvas corners: device launcher applies the mask. Output only ONE final square icon, no mockup or design board.
