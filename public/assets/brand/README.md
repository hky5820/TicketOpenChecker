# Ticket Open icon

Original artwork: `ticket-open-master.png`, revised with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-29. White background, a horizontal navy ticket and a clear clock replace the previous blue, tilted design. No external logo or stock artwork is used.

Run `node scripts/make-icons.js` from the repository root to regenerate the six PNG assets with installed Chrome. The 192/512px general icons and 16/32px favicons have transparent rounded corners. The 180px Apple touch icon and 512px Android maskable icon retain opaque, full-bleed backgrounds so the OS can apply its own shape. The header and notification icons use the same artwork.

Android artwork must remain inside the central circle with radius 40% of image width: [maskable icon guidance](https://web.dev/articles/maskable-icon). The PNGs are committed; deployment does not need image generation.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Redesign the referenced Ticket Open app icon into a much cleaner, immediately understandable WHITE app icon. Preserve only its core idea: ticket reservations opening at a particular time. Replace the entire blue background with a perfectly flat, clean warm-white #FAFAF8 full-bleed square. Replace the tilted white ticket with ONE centered, perfectly horizontal, bold solid dark navy #24334C admission-ticket silhouette. Give the silhouette generous softly rounded corners and a clear semicircular inward notch midway along BOTH left and right edges. Integrate one simple white clock dial into the larger left section of the ticket: a white circular cutout with only TWO thick dark navy clock hands making an unmistakable L at 3 o'clock (one pointing up and one pointing right). Do not draw tick marks, numbers or an additional center dot. Separate the narrow right ticket stub with only TWO short white rounded vertical perforation slots, balanced above and below the center, large enough to survive 24px. Geometry must be impeccably crisp, balanced, and restrained; treat it as premium Swiss graphic design with just two flat colors and large negative space. Make the complete navy ticket fit comfortably within a centered circle whose radius is 36 percent of the image width, about 65 percent width by 40 percent height, optically centered. The background must stay predominantly white. No tilt, no gradient anywhere, no 3D, no shadows, no paper texture, no embossing, no border around the app tile, no sparkle, no badges, no text or letters, no decorative elements, no glossy effects. Full square canvas with NO rounded outer corners: device launchers will apply their own mask. Deliver only ONE finished high-resolution square production app icon, no mockup, no comparison board. Reference image is a semantic reference only; the new icon should be dramatically calmer, simpler, whiter and more legible.
