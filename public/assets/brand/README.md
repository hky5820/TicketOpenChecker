# Ticket Open icon

Original artwork: `ticket-open-master.png`, generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-29. The new opening-ticket emblem combines a bold cobalt loop, ticket notches and a lifted periwinkle corner on white. It replaces the literal ticket-and-clock design. No external logo or stock artwork is used.

Run `node scripts/make-icons.js` from the repository root to regenerate the six PNG assets with installed Chrome. The 192/512px general icons and 16/32px favicons have transparent rounded corners. Apple touch and Android maskable icons retain opaque square backgrounds so the OS can apply its own shape. The Android version adds 6% white padding on each edge to protect the lifted corner; smaller icons retain the larger mark for legibility. Header and notifications use the same artwork.

Android artwork must remain inside the central circle with radius 40% of image width: [maskable icon guidance](https://web.dev/articles/maskable-icon). The PNGs are committed; deployment does not need image generation.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design an original, exceptionally refined app icon for Ticket Open, a ticket sale opening tracker. This is a fresh brand concept, not a ticket clip-art illustration. Creative idea: an OPENING TICKET. Create ONE large, bold electric-cobalt-blue folded ribbon emblem on a pure soft-white #FAFAFC square background. The emblem should combine the impression of a ticket and an opening doorway into a beautiful compact abstract O: a thick, softly squared loop with a generous white center and one deliberate opening at its upper-right corner, where a short ticket flap lifts forward. One or two subtle concave ticket notches may be incorporated into the outer silhouette only if they enhance the logo. The visual should read as one coherent, confident, memorable logo, not as two unrelated objects. Main color vivid #3154F5; the single folded return face may be light periwinkle blue #9DB5FF. Make the transition geometric and clean, not a gradient. Keep an equal balance of visual weight, large clean negative space and luxurious precision. Pure white background dominates; the saturated emblem provides immediate recognition on a phone screen. Think expertly art-directed Japanese / Swiss contemporary identity design: warm rounded geometry with one decisive, distinctive cut. Absolutely NO clock, calendar, bell, checkmark, lettering, numbers, dotted perforation, sparkles, stars, badges, outlines, border around the icon, glass, shadows, glow, bevels, paper texture or busy micro details. No thin strokes. Do not show a conventional long horizontal admission ticket. Avoid a generic abstract infinity logo. The mark should occupy approximately 62 percent width and 62 percent height, all essential artwork strictly inside a central circle of radius 37 percent of canvas width. Clean flat vector-like solid color geometry, optically centered. Full-bleed opaque WHITE square canvas with NO rounded outer corners, one final standalone app icon only, no mockup, no design board, no captions. It must look intentional, attractive and unmistakably sharp at 24 and 48 pixels.
