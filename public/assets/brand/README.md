# Ticket Open icon

Original artwork: `ticket-open-master.png`, generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-29. The white-background cobalt admission ticket carries a large white OPEN wordmark so the ticket-opening meaning is explicit. Side notches establish the ticket silhouette; the only interior content is OPEN. No external logo, stock artwork or font file is used.

Run `node scripts/make-icons.js` from the repository root to regenerate the six PNG assets with installed Chrome. The 192/512px general icons and 16/32px favicons have transparent rounded corners. Apple touch and Android maskable icons retain opaque square backgrounds so the OS can apply its own shape. The Android version adds 6% white padding on each edge to protect the ticket corners; smaller icons retain the larger mark for legibility. Header and notifications use the same artwork.

Android artwork must remain inside the central circle with radius 40% of image width: [maskable icon guidance](https://web.dev/articles/maskable-icon). The PNGs are committed; deployment does not need image generation.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Redesign the referenced Ticket Open app icon to communicate the specific idea TICKET SALES ARE OPEN in the most direct, immediately understandable way. The previous bell communicates generic notifications and must be removed completely. New concept: one beautiful bold admission ticket bearing the single large word OPEN. Keep the clean white background and vivid cobalt-blue brand palette. ONE solid cobalt #3154F5 ticket silhouette, with rounded outer corners and unmistakable generous semicircular notches in the middle of BOTH left and right edges. Make the ticket a compact landscape shape approximately 1.5 times as wide as tall, slightly inclined upward to the right by 6 degrees. The ticket itself is the only foreground object. Within the ticket put only the exact uppercase word "OPEN", spelled O-P-E-N, all on ONE line, in extremely large confident white rounded geometric sans-serif lettering, tightly and beautifully typeset, optically centered. The letters must occupy most of the usable ticket width and about half of its height; this is a striking typographic app mark, not small printed ticket detail. Think a premium contemporary app identity: powerful blue silhouette, extremely crisp white word OPEN, elegant proportions and generous white breathing room outside. A tiny light-blue top-right fold is allowed only if it stays unobtrusive, but the word OPEN must be the dominant unmistakable message. Do not add any other text, numbers, barcode, perforation, lines, icons or decoration. Absolutely NO clock, bell, calendar, checkmark, abstract O emblem, arrow, sparkle, badge, extra torn stub, shadow, texture, gradient, bevel or 3D. Do not use a generic OPEN shop sign with a hanging string; this is clearly an admission ticket. Center the complete mark and keep its dimensions around 70 percent canvas width by 48 percent canvas height, with every edge within a centered circle of radius 42 percent canvas width. Full-bleed opaque WHITE square canvas, no rounded outer canvas corners, no colored tile behind the ticket. One finished polished high-resolution square app icon only, no mockup, no presentation board. The word OPEN must be legible at home-screen icon sizes.
