# Ticket Open icon

Original artwork: `ticket-open-master.png`, generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-09-29. No external logo or stock artwork is used.

Run `node scripts/make-icons.js` from the repository root to regenerate the six PNG assets with installed Chrome. The 192/512px general icons and 16/32px favicons have transparent rounded corners. The 180px Apple touch icon and 512px Android maskable icon retain opaque, full-bleed backgrounds so the OS can apply its own shape. The header and notification icons use the same artwork.

Android artwork must remain inside the central circle with radius 40% of image width: [maskable icon guidance](https://web.dev/articles/maskable-icon). The PNGs are committed; deployment does not need image generation.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE finished square app icon for a Korean ticket-opening schedule app called Ticket Open. It tracks when concert tickets and baseball tickets go on sale. Deliver ONLY the app icon, filling the complete square canvas edge to edge, no mockup, no display sheet, no device, no words or letters, no watermark. At least 1024 by 1024. Background is a rich vivid cobalt blue, matching #3655cf, with a very restrained blue tonal gradient, full bleed and NO rounded outer corners; the phone launcher will apply its own shape. Foreground: a bold, beautifully proportioned ivory-white admission ticket, slightly tilted counterclockwise, with unmistakable concave ticket notches at the left and right ends. A single dark cobalt clock dial is precisely inset into the large left section of the ticket, with only two thick hands and NO numerals or tick marks. The clock should be immediately readable as time at 32px. Small clean perforation separation near the right quarter of the ticket, only two or three bold short slots. Premium minimal graphic design: a strong silhouette, generous breathing room, refined geometry, crisp contours, sophisticated proportion, almost flat vector-like rendering with at most a subtle layered-paper edge, no glossy plastic, no neon glow, no sparkle, no busy textures, no ornamental rings, no extraneous badges or notification dot. Keep the entire white ticket silhouette and every essential detail within the CENTER 72% circle of the canvas, for Android circular mask safety, while maintaining generous icon scale. The icon must remain distinctive and legible when shrunk to 25px. One unified ticket-with-clock symbol only, horizontally oriented, balanced optically at the center. This is a production app launcher icon, not a conceptual illustration.
